Matthew Prall

I’m a senior software engineer working on site reliability at Microsoft. I’ve been here since 2016, mostly on Azure systems in government and sovereign clouds, and in the public cloud as well. My days are reliability automation, observability, and incident response. I like a pipeline that does the careful thing the same way every time.

I studied mechanical engineering at Purdue: a BSME in 2015 and an MSME in 2016, focused on design, manufacturing, and materials. Summers at Boeing included composite fabrication for the 787 and 777X, and a summer on the Space Launch System at NASA’s Michoud Assembly Facility. I live in the Snoqualmie, Washington area, and I coach a FIRST LEGO League team.

Factory Telemetry Simulator

  • Python
  • PyScript

Python runs in the browser, via PyScript, and simulates four factory machines streaming sensor data. A reorder buffer puts out-of-order messages back in sequence, and a rolling z-score detector with hysteresis raises alarms. You can inject bearing wear, overheating, sensor dropout, a noise spike, or network delay, and then clear the fault.
